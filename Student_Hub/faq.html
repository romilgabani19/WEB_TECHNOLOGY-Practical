Frequently Asked Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online portal where students can register, view events, manage their profiles, and receive college updates.

How can I register?

Open the Register page, fill in your details, and click the Register button. You can register by clicking on the "Register" button in the navigation bar and filling out the required information.

How do I log in?

Go to the Login page and enter your registered email address and password.

Can I update my profile?

Yes. Visit the Profile page to view and update your personal information.Yes, you can update your profile information at any time by navigating to the Profile page and making the necessary changes.

How do I register for college events?

Visit the Events page and click the Register button for the event you wish to Romil.

How can I contact the college?

Go to the Contact page and send your message using the contact form.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact the college administrator to reset your account password.
